# Special compatibility

- Foliax: arcane infusion skips void data disks (they are item-with-tags)
- rigor-module: no rigor copies of part 3 recipes
- Any mod: recipes that raise quality without a quality_max, and items that spoil into a higher quality, stop at legendary
- Any mod: recipes with a fixed Void product (Maraxsis promethium quality step 5) are hidden
- Rabbasca underground: imaginary items are not learnable (it keeps them out of other mods' recipes)
- xy K2SO enhancements: loads first, so the Void quality takes its legendary science pack drain
- Any mod: add learnable items through the voidp-learnable-items mod-data
